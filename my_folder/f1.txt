In the hush of dawn, the world awakes,
Whispers of the wind, the gentle breaks,
Softly dancing leaves, a graceful sway,
Nature's symphony begins the day.

The sun peeks through the curtain of night,
Casting golden rays, a warm, soft light,
Birds take to the sky, a joyful flight,
Songs of freedom echo, pure delight.

Meadows dressed in dew, like diamonds bright,
Flowers bloom in colors, a stunning sight,
A brook babbles softly, secrets to share,
With every ripple, whispers fill the air.

Mountains stand tall, guardians of time,
Their peaks kissed by clouds, a realm sublime,
The forest breathes deep, ancient and wise,
In the heart of the woods, tranquility lies.

As shadows grow long, and twilight descends,
The day meets the night, as time gently bends,
Stars twinkle above, like dreams in the sky,
A canvas of hope where wishes can fly.
